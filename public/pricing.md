# Reader access and pricing state

Reader currently has no public plan, checkout, paid tier, or purchase flow.
The repository also does not establish a permanent free promise.

## Current access

- The browser-local path can be used without a Reader account.
- Google sign-in opens the account-backed personal library.
- The production posture is personal-use and maintenance-first.
- There is no public team or organization plan.

## What this page does not claim

This page does not invent a price, free-forever promise, trial, subscription,
refund policy, billing API, or future launch date. If Reader's commercial state
changes, this document and the human landing page must change together.
